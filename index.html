01 — Who We Are

Cortec® Middle East is the regional office of Cortec® Corporation — the global leader in environmentally responsible corrosion control. From Jebel Ali, our engineers protect the assets that power the region.

Since 1977 · St. Paul → Dubai

What started in a small garage in St. Paul, Minnesota has grown into a global network of manufacturing, research and engineering. Across the GCC and North Africa, we pair that technology with local expertise — from specification and on-site application to long-term monitoring.

03 — Products

Two technologies. Thousands of applications.

Patented inhibitor chemistries that protect metal and reinforced concrete — without heavy coatings, hazardous solvents or downtime.

VpCI®

Vapor phase Corrosion InhibitorsProtection that reaches every surface — even the hidden ones.

VpCI® molecules vaporise and form a mono-molecular protective layer on metal surfaces inside enclosed spaces, packaging and process systems.

Sustainability

Corrosion protection that doesn't cost the planet.

Cortec® pioneered environmentally responsible corrosion control. Our products help asset owners cut waste, solvents and maintenance — while extending the life of what's already built.

↗ Bio-based formulas

Renewable, plant-derived raw materials such as soy-based EcoLine®.

↗ Recyclable packaging

Recyclable and compostable films, including EcoWorks®.

↗ Low VOC

Water-based coatings that reduce hazardous emissions on site.

↗ Longer asset life

Less replacement steel and concrete means a lower carbon footprint.
